How it started

Baked with love,
rooted in Seoul.

Sora's Bake Studio began in 2021 with a simple idea: bake like you're feeding family. Our founder grew up watching her grandmother shape red bean buns before sunrise in a small Seoul kitchen — and that warmth is what we bring to Melbourne every single morning.

We use no preservatives, no shortcuts. Just good flour, real butter, and seasonal ingredients sourced from Melbourne markets. Everything in our cabinet was baked this morning.

Sora means "sky" in Korean — a reminder to always aim higher, and to fill each day with something beautiful, one bake at a time.
