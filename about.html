Our story

A family recipe,
every morning.

Sora's Bake Studio began in 2021 with a simple idea: bake like you're feeding family. Our founder grew up watching her grandmother shape red bean buns before sunrise in a small Seoul kitchen — and that warmth is what we bring to Melbourne every single morning.

How we work

No shortcuts.
No exceptions.

We use no preservatives, no artificial flavourings, no frozen dough. Just good flour, real butter, and seasonal ingredients sourced from Melbourne's best markets. Every recipe has been tested hundreds of times before it makes it onto our menu.

Sora means "sky" in Korean — a reminder to always aim higher, and to fill each day with something worth waking up for.

"I don't want anyone to eat our cakes and feel like they came from a factory. Every single one should feel like it was made just for them."

— Sora, Founder
