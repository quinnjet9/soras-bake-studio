Our Story

A family recipe, every morning.

Haru Bakery House began in 2021 with a simple idea: bake like you're feeding family. Our founder grew up watching her grandmother shape red bean buns before sunrise in a small Seoul kitchen — and that warmth is what we bring to Melbourne every single day.

We use no preservatives, no shortcuts. Just good flour, real butter, and seasonal ingredients sourced locally. Everything you see in our cabinet was baked this morning.

Haru means "day" in Korean — a reminder that the best things are worth doing daily, with full attention and care.
